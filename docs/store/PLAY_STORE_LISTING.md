## Full description

Your movement becomes a place you can return to.

YourLeft records outdoor runs, walks, and rides, then turns the route into a replay through a stylized 3D miniature world. Keep the phone away while you move; come back afterward to explore the route, review your activity, and share a clean route graphic or photo story.

**Record outdoors**
- Capture a walk, run, or cycle with GPS.
- Review distance, duration, pace or speed, and the recorded route.
- Android recording can continue with an ongoing location notification when the screen is locked.

**Replay your route**
- Move around the miniature world and revisit the route from different views.
- See roads, buildings, terrain, and greenery where map data is available.
- Export an activity backup as GeoJSON.

**Make it yours**
- Create a transparent route image or a moving route story.
- Choose what activity details appear before sharing.
- YourLeft does not require an account. Activities are stored on your device; map providers receive the approximate area needed to load geography.

GPS and map availability vary by device, reception, network, and data coverage. Distances, pace, speed, elevation, and map geometry are estimates and are not for navigation or medical use. Stay aware of your surroundings and do not use the app while moving through traffic.
